# 01 Product scope and MVP

1. (BLOCKER) What is the MVP boundary? List which of the reference modules are IN for 1.0, which are simplified, which are deferred.
2. (BLOCKER) What is the first target customer segment for launch: small private schools (100-500 students), mid schools, academies/coaching centers? Which one do we optimise the first release for?
3. Do we launch city-wide/nationally or pilot with a few institutions first? How many pilot institutions, and who are they?
4. What is the product name, brand tone and domain strategy (one domain with tenant subdomains, or per-school custom domains later)?
5. What is the single sentence promise to (a) the owner, (b) the teacher, (c) the parent, (d) the student?
6. What are the top 5 pains of Pakistani institutions you want 1.0 to clearly solve better than the reference?
7. Success metrics for 1.0: activation (school sets up in X days), weekly active parents %, fee on-time %, schools retained after trial %, support tickets per school. Which ones and what targets?
8. Which engagement features are in 1.0 versus later (digital ID, weekly parent digest, appreciation badges, streaks)?
9. Is a public marketing website/landing page and self-serve signup part of 1.0?
10. What is explicitly OUT of scope for 1.0 (so no one builds it by accident)?
11. Do we need an Urdu/Roman-Urdu content option inside messages even though the UI is English only?
12. What is the minimum offline capability promised in 1.0 (see section 05 and 11)?
13. What is the release plan: one big 1.0 or staged (1.0 core admin + fees + attendance, 1.1 exams, 1.2 AI insights)?
