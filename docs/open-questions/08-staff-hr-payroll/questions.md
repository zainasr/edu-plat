# 08 Staff, HR and payroll

1. (BLOCKER) Is payroll in 1.0 or only staff management + attendance? (It is listed in MVP: staff management, finance.) How deep: monthly fixed salary only, or per-lecture/hourly for academy teachers?
2. Staff record fields: confirm list (CNIC, DOB, contact, address, qualification, experience, designation, department, joining date, contract type, bank account, emergency contact, documents). Mandatory vs optional?
3. Staff types and designations: fixed type list (reference) vs configurable designations/departments?
4. Teaching assignments: class teacher, subject-teacher per section, workload limits, substitute teacher flow when absent.
5. Salary structure: basic + allowances (house, transport, medical) - deductions (late, absent, advance, tax, EOBI, provident fund, security). Which are in 1.0?
6. Pay cycle: monthly, date, proration for joiners/leavers, unpaid leave calculation rule (per working day or per 30 days)?
7. Payment methods and payslips: cash, bank transfer file (which banks' formats), payslip PDF/WhatsApp. Approval workflow (draft -> review -> approve -> pay)?
8. Advances and loans: needed in 1.0?
9. Security deduction policy (new staff, returned on leaving) as in reference: keep?
10. Leave management: leave types, yearly quota, carry forward, who approves (principal, admin), half-day, short leave, balance display in app?
11. Performance and appraisal: any in 1.0 (teacher ratings, class results as indicator, parent feedback)? Or later via analytics?
12. Recruitment: staff job application form (reference template). Needed in 1.0?
13. Staff app features: attendance check-in, timetable, mark attendance/marks, leave request, payslip view, announcements, chats with parents. Confirm scope.
14. Staff exit: notice, final settlement, certificates (experience letter).
