1: [accepted] Roles: Owner (one or more per institution, billing + everything + can created or remove admins), Admin = admin of online portal can see/do anything with the portal although cannot remove owner, Teacher, Receptionist, Accountant (fees/finance + teacher thier own attendence,thier management activities), Student, Parent, plus Custom roles ADDED (2026-10-19): roles are additive building blocks, not job titles; schools may combine them freely per person.
2: [can be any model that is easy to manage and scale and well engineered and proven in systems] Permission flags (module.action) grouped into roles. Built-in roles ship with sensible defaults; Owner can clone and edit them into custom roles. About 60-80 flags (view/create/edit/delete/approve/export per module plus special: void_payment, give_discount, approve_salary, view_finance_reports).
3: [ACCEPTED] Owner decision (2026-10-19): in Pakistani institutions one person often does several jobs (e.g. accountant + receptionist + teacher). One user identity (phone) can hold MANY roles at once in the same institution, and effective permissions are the UNION of all assigned roles (and of their scopes) at all times, with no role switching needed inside one institution. A context switcher exists only between different institutions or organisations. Maker-checker still applies: one person holding both maker and checker roles cannot approve their own action.
4: [accepted] Yes. Scope = whole institution / branch(es) / assigned classes+sections / own. Class teacher and subject teacher are assignments, not roles.
