# 07 Answers

1: [ACCEPTED] Build the double-entry posting engine from day one (auto-posts every fee, payment, expense, salary), but expose only plain-language screens in 1.0 (collections, expenses, cash book, owner reports). Journal/ledger/trial balance views are visible only to the Accountant/Owner "Advanced" area. Primary users: owner (reports), receptionist/accountant (collections, expenses). REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference shows a hidden double-entry ledger (380 ledger entries in the first year) behind plain-language Financial Reports with an Owner Dashboard, plus accountant-level Trial Balance, General Ledger, Journal and Chart of Accounts.
2: [ACCEPTED] Configurable per institution with a default of July-June (typical for Pakistani academic sessions); reports can also be filtered by academic session.
3: [ACCEPTED] Owner views default to Cash basis (money in/out) with an Accrual toggle for accountants.
4: [ACCEPTED] Fixed system chart (codes locked), names renameable, custom sub-accounts added under expenses and income; cash/bank accounts added by admin. Cash book per branch. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Chart of Accounts page: fixed books plus the cash and bank accounts behind them (e.g. Cash in Hand); payments name the account they are deposited to.
5: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: categories, vendor (optional text), paid-now or on-credit with later settlement, attachment (photo of bill), single-level approval above a threshold. Recurring templates and petty cash module in 1.1.
6: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: generic "Other income" entry with category. Dedicated donations/canteen/rentals modules deferred.
7: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: each collector has a cash session (opening, collections, closing count, variance note) and a "day close" report; deposit to bank recorded as transfer between cash and bank. Variance requires note.
8: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: manual bank entries with reference; statement import and auto-match in 1.1.
9: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: Owner dashboard, Collection report (day/month/class), Defaulters and aging, P&L (cash or accrual), Cash flow, Daily cash book, Expense analysis, Concession report, Payroll summary. DEFER: bridge analysis, seasonality, unit economics, equity, balance sheet (kept in engine, UI in 1.1+), fixed assets. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference report catalogue (24 reports in 7 groups): Start here (Owner Dashboard, Unit Economics); Financial statements (Profit and Loss with cash or accrual, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Changes in Equity); Comparison and planning (Comparison, Budget vs Actual, Bridge Analysis, Seasonality and Trend); Receivables and collections (Receivables Aging, Defaulters, Fee Collections, Collections, Concessions and Yield); Payroll and operations (Payroll, Expense Analysis, Fixed Assets); Ledger and controls (Trial Balance, General Ledger, Journal, Period Close, Chart of Accounts). Mimic decision: 1.0 ships Owner Dashboard, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Receivables Aging, Defaulters, Fee Collections, Collections, Concessions and Yield, Payroll, Expense Analysis, Trial Balance, General Ledger, Journal, Period Close, Chart of Accounts. 1.1: Comparison, Changes in Equity, Budget vs Actual, Bridge Analysis, Seasonality, Unit Economics, Fixed Assets.
10: [ACCEPTED] Later (1.2): annual budget vs actual. Not in 1.0.
11: [ACCEPTED] Month close lock (Owner/Accountant), postings to a closed month are blocked with an adjustment entry in the next open month (as reference). Manual journals only for Accountant role, always audited. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Period Close page: freeze a month once reported; later postings roll forward rather than restating it. Journal page shows every entry and where manual ones are posted. Needs Attention page flags accounting entries that failed to post.
12: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: per-branch books with consolidated owner view; inter-branch transfers recorded as simple transfers. No intercompany eliminations.
13: [ACCEPTED] Excel and PDF exports for all reports; no third-party accounting connectors in 1.0 (Tally/QuickBooks export file in a later release if demanded).
14: [ACCEPTED] Later (1.2+). Account codes for fixed assets exist in chart but no UI in 1.0. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference includes Fixed Assets (asset register, net book value, monthly depreciation run) inside Financial Reports. Keep as 1.1 given simplicity, but the chart of accounts and report slot are reserved.
15: [ACCEPTED] None built in 1.0; present clean reports that accountants can use. Ask a Pakistani chartered accountant to review during design phase for tax treatment, trust/non-profit schools, and audit formats.
