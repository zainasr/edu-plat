# 19 Future phases (so 1.0 design does not block them)

1. Online paper/test maker: scope vision (teacher builds papers from a question bank, auto-generated papers by syllabus/difficulty, print and online delivery, student exam prep, auto-marking, analytics feeding improvement)? Who creates the question bank (us, schools, AI-assisted)? Copyright/licensing of textbook content?
2. Student exam prep product: separate subscription for students/parents (B2C) or bundled into school plans?
3. Colleges/universities: which features differ (semesters, credit hours, GPA/CGPA, course registration, department/faculty hierarchy, transcripts, hostel, library)? Timing and how much data model must anticipate now?
4. Transport module (routes, stops, fees, GPS), library, hostel, inventory/store, canteen: which are likely next, and the order?
5. Alumni and fundraising? Parent community/forums?
6. Parent payments beyond fees (uniform shop, trips, events) — marketplace potential?
7. Learning content: any plan for light LMS (assignments submissions, quizzes) despite "not an LMS"? Where is the line?
8. Hardware: biometric/face devices, smart gates, ID card printing partnerships: bundled offering?
9. Marketplace/partners: banks, insurance, book/uniform vendors, ed-fin (fee financing for parents, school loans using our data)?
10. Multi-language UI (Urdu) later? Timeline and implications for design now (string externalisation, RTL layout readiness)?
11. International expansion (Bangladesh, Nepal, Gulf expats)? Currency, languages, tax, calendar implications to avoid hardcoding?
12. Public APIs and developer ecosystem?
13. Government/board reporting integrations and accreditation (long term)?
14. Data products: anonymised insights for policymakers/publishers (ethical limits)?
