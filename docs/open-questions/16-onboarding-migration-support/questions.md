# 16 Onboarding, data migration and support

1. (BLOCKER) What does a new school's first week look like? Target time from signup to first fee collected and first attendance marked. Who does the heavy lifting (school, our team, agent)?
2. Data import: which entities in 1.0 (students+families, staff, classes/sections/subjects, fee plans, opening dues, historical attendance/results?) and file format (Excel .xlsx, CSV)? Templates, validation, preview, error report, rollback/undo of a bad import?
3. Where do schools keep data today (Excel, paper registers, other software)? How messy (duplicate names, missing mobiles, inconsistent classes)? Do we offer data-cleaning service?
4. Opening balances: dues carried from the old system/paper; cash and bank starting balances; staff advances. How captured and verified?
5. Setup wizard order: institution profile, branches/shifts, session, classes/sections/subjects, fee plans, staff, students, parents invited, go-live checklist. Anything to reorder?
6. Parent activation: how do we get 70%+ parents on the app (SMS/WhatsApp invites, QR poster, school-assisted)? Track and nudge non-activated families?
7. Training: videos, in-app guides, live sessions, role-based cheat sheets, certification for school admins? Language (Urdu videos acceptable even if UI English)?
8. Demo/sandbox tenant for sales and training with realistic fake data?
9. Support channels and SLAs (see section 13). Escalation levels? Remote assistance tools?
10. Switching from competitors: import from their exports; migration fee policy?
11. Go-live checks and readiness score per tenant (data completeness, staff accounts, parents invited)?
12. Offboarding: export package (Excel/PDF/zip of documents), certificates retained, exit survey.
13. Feedback loop: in-app feedback, feature voting, pilot school reviews, release notes shown in-app?
14. Community and partner: school associations, agents, local meetups for acquisition: out of scope for product but affects features (referral tracking)?
