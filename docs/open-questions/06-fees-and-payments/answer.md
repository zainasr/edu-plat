# 06 Answers

1: [FEE STRUCTURE IS DEPENDED INSTITUE TO TO INSTITUE WE GIVE DEFAULT ONE BUT IT IS CUSTOMIZABLE AT INSTITUTE TO INSTITUE] Fee Plan per class (list of fee heads with amounts and frequency: monthly, one-time at admission, annual, ad hoc). Student-level override per head and discount. Heads: tuition, admission, registration, annual/exam fee, transport, lab, library, sports, fine, security deposit, custom. Academies: per-course/batch fee with the same mechanism (1.1).
2: [INSTITURE TO INSTITUTE] Monthly default; per institution option for Quarterly/Term/Annual cycles (frequency on the fee head, not on the whole plan). 1.0: monthly + one-time + annual; term/quarterly in 1.1.
3: [ACCEPTED AND INSTITUTE CUSTOMIZABLE] Keep draft-review-release. Auto-draft on the 25th for next month (configurable), release by admin/accountant in bulk with exceptions list (new admissions, discount changes, mid-month joiners), due date = 10th (configurable). Parents see only released challans.
4: [ACCEPTED] Late fee options per institution: none / fixed per challan / fixed per day / percent, with cap; applied automatically on the day after due date as a separate fee line; waiver by permission with reason (maker-checker above threshold).
5: [ACCEPTED] 1.0 types: sibling, staff-child, scholarship (fixed or %), concession (need-based), custom. Each has scope (tuition only or all heads), duration (until date/session), approval flag, report. Stacking rule: apply highest single discount unless institution allows stacking (default: max of discounts, no stacking).
6: [ACCEPTED] Owner decision (2026-10-19): follow the reference. An advance/overpayment is simply payment against a future challan (shown as Advance), not a free-floating family credit balance. Any unallocated amount must be applied to a challan or refunded explicitly.
7: [ACCEPTED] Yes: "Opening balance / remaining dues" entered per student at import or manually, tracked as a dated receivable that appears on challans and in aging reports. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Fee collection has a "Remaining Dues" fee type and a per-student outstanding list including old overdue amounts (e.g. 180-270 days), so opening dues are tracked and collectable.
8: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: cash, bank deposit/transfer (manual entry with reference), cheque, plus JazzCash/Easypaisa/1Link or Raast QR through ONE aggregator/payment gateway chosen in design phase, settling to the school's bank account. Cards later. Online is optional per school. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference records counter payments (method "not stated" at the counter, posted into a named account such as Cash in Hand) and has an optional Online Payments setup that is OFF by default ("parents cannot pay online"). Mimic: 1.0 ships counter/bank/cheque with account selection per payment; online gateway is an opt-in setup per school (our aggregator), not required to go live. Add a "payment method" field (the reference leaves it "not stated").
9: [ACCEPTED] Each challan gets a unique consumer/voucher number (1Bill/1Link-style). Online payments reconcile by webhook; bank slips by manual entry or CSV bank statement upload with auto-match (1.1). Unmatched payments go to an "unallocated" queue.
10: [ACCEPTED] Receipts numbered per institution per fiscal year (e.g. REC-2026-000123), sequential, no gaps; formats A4, A5, 80 mm; family receipt lists sibling lines; voiding keeps the number, requires reason and approval, produces a reversing entry; reprint marks "Duplicate". REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Receipt number format is PREFIX-YEAR-SEQUENCE (6-digit, per year) and is configurable under Fee Settings > Invoice Settings; payments list has a "Show voided" filter, so voiding exists and voided receipts stay visible. Family payments show one receipt with "+N siblings, N fee lines".
11: [ACCEPTED] Reminder schedule configurable: 3 days before, due day, +3, +7, +15 (parent app push, WhatsApp/SMS). Escalation to owner list at +30. Policy options per school (off by default): hide report card, flag at gate, auto-status "Defaulter". No automatic strike-off. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference defines Defaulters as 30+ days overdue; Collect screen has tabs All outstanding / Overdue / Partial / Defaulters / Family billing and bulk "select overdue/defaulters". Keep 30 days as default threshold, configurable.
12: [ACCEPTED] Security held as liability; at withdrawal shown for adjust or refund; refund requires approval and creates ledger entries; refunds paid in cash/bank recorded. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Security Deposit is a fee type marked Refundable "held on the family's behalf, returned when the student leaves"; it is shown as held money not income and has its own Security Deposits page for refunds.
13: [ACCEPTED] Plan changes apply from an effective month; released challans are never changed (adjustment line instead); drafts regenerate. Audit trail of plan changes.
14: [ACCEPTED] Yes: family statement across branches/shifts, one payment allocated across children (per rule 6), per-child receipts plus a combined receipt. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference has a Family billing tab (81 families) and family payments covering several students; confirmed as first-class.
15: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: only simple extra fee heads (books/uniform as ad hoc fees); no inventory or shop. Donations/fund as an income head in 1.1. REFERENCE EVIDENCE (Edflo hands-on 2026-10-12): Reference fee types include Transport, Library, Sports, Laboratory, Examination, Event, Fine, Other and an Additional Fees page (fines, exams, events); school can add its own types and switch types off. No inventory/shop. Mimic: configurable fee-type catalogue with defaults that are suggestions, never locks.
16: [ACCEPTED] Parent sees: family dues summary, per-child breakdown, due dates, history, downloadable receipts/challans, pay now (if online enabled), payment instructions (bank/accounts), payment status in real time; pay-all or select challans.
17: [ACCEPTED] 1.0: configurable NTN and tax lines are not generated; schools remain responsible. Revisit with a local accountant during design (education-sector tax treatment, withholding on online payments).
